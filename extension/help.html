VideoDrop

Your video, in a few clicks.

Make it easy to reach

Click Chrome’s puzzle-piece Extensions icon, then pin VideoDrop. You can also press Alt + Shift + V. If that shortcut is in use, choose one at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Download a video

  1. Open a page and play the video.
  2. Click the VideoDrop icon and choose a video or quality from the dropdown.
  3. Click Download. Your file goes to Downloads.

You can close the popup while a download runs. Open it again to see progress or cancel. Turn on “Ask where to save” in Settings if you prefer a save dialog.

YouTube & MP3

Open a public YouTube video, click VideoDrop, choose a resolution or Audio only · MP3, and click Download. Tracks are fetched in full and joined or converted inside Chrome. No helper, installer, account login, or remote conversion service is required. The combined input limit is 512 MB; choose a lower resolution for longer videos. Unavailable videos and live YouTube broadcasts are not supported.

What you can save

Direct MP4, WebM, MOV, M4V and OGV files, plus unencrypted HLS and finished DASH videos. Pick a quality; separate audio and video tracks are fetched completely and merged on your computer. HLS/MP4 streams are normally saved as .mp4; WebM DASH stays .webm. If the original codecs require another container, VideoDrop can preserve them in .mkv. Original video quality is retained without re-encoding.

Local stream processing runs one job at a time, with a 512 MB media limit. Regular files use Chrome’s downloader. If a site refuses Chrome’s direct request, VideoDrop automatically retries once using the page’s original referrer and cookies; this local recovery path has the same 512 MB limit.

Live HLS streams

Click Download to record the segments still available from the source and fetch new ones as they arrive. The job shows Recording stream. Click Finish & save to keep the captured portion, or Cancel to stop. When the source ends its playlist, VideoDrop finishes automatically. It cannot recover earlier broadcast segments the server has already removed.

Nothing in the dropdown?

Start playback and click Rescan. If you installed or reloaded VideoDrop while this page was already open, reload the page once. Chrome internal pages and the Chrome Web Store do not allow extension scanning.

VideoDrop supports public YouTube videos in this extension. There is no separate desktop program or helper installer. Encrypted/DRM media, live DASH, multi-period DASH and sources requiring special authorization headers are not supported. Some sites expose only a browser-local blob without a usable source. Expired links can require replaying the video and scanning again.

Privacy & permissions

VideoDrop runs on your device. No account, analytics, advertising, remote code, or upload service is included. It observes media response headers and video elements on HTTP/HTTPS pages so videos can appear before you open the menu. Detected addresses and recent download statuses stay in Chrome’s session storage and clear when the browser restarts. Preferences are stored locally.

Website access is needed for embedded players and media/CDN domains. Download permission saves files; an offscreen document and bundled media worker join audio/video locally. Temporary request rules restore the original page referrer for the extension’s own media fetches and are removed afterward. Requests go to the video’s own servers and may include normal site cookies. Chrome applies its usual download safety checks.

Patreon and Ko-fi open only when clicked. You can hide them in Settings.
